Powerups

Unleash Devastating Blasts

Cross Blast

Place a crystal with a cross powerup and watch it detonate -- clearing the entire intersecting row and column in a single satisfying blast.

Trigger a shockwave that shatters every block in a 3x3 zone. Perfect for clearing tight spots and creating space when you need it most.

Activate the reticle, take aim, and erase any single block on the board. A precision tool for surgical plays when every cell counts.

Every session is unique with randomized pieces and powerup chances.
